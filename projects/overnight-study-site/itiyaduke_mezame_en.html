Staying Awake

Rest when you feel very sleepy, and avoid excessive caffeine intake.

I would like to share with you some ways to stay awake during an overnight soak.

Caffeinated drinks can increase alertness, but the amount of caffeine varies by product. Timing and individual sensitivity can also affect later sleep.

Excessive intake can cause adverse effects such as dizziness, a rapid heartbeat, anxiety, insomnia or nausea. Check product labels and public-health guidance rather than combining multiple caffeinated drinks.

The second is to vocalize.Solving calculations while singing a song or memorizing while speaking aloud will help you stay awake and improve your study efficiency.

Lastly, you have to stand up and study.If you sit and study all the time, you will always have a strong sleeping spell.So, if you feel sleepy, stand up and study.
