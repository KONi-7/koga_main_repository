About Overnight Study

Prioritise adequate sleep for your health and learning. This page is content for a web-development exercise.

Overnight cramming means trying to cover a large amount of material immediately before an exam. It may feel time-efficient, but it reduces sleep and can make it harder to concentrate.

A more reliable approach is to identify the most important topics early, divide them into small tasks and leave time for review.

If preparation starts late, prioritise the key concepts instead of trying to memorise everything. Short breaks and a clear stopping time can also help prevent exhaustion.

This page keeps the original theme of the project while presenting it as a web-development exercise rather than health or academic advice.
