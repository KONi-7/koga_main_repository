How to Plan a Study Session

Prioritise adequate sleep for your health and learning. This page is content for a web-development exercise.

Begin by listing the topics and ranking them by importance. Assign a realistic amount of time to each task, including breaks.

Concentration is easier to maintain in short, focused sessions. During breaks, step away from the screen, drink water or move around before returning to the next task.

For memory-based subjects, use active recall: close the material and explain the idea in your own words. For mathematics and science, solve practice problems and review the steps behind mistakes.

When a topic remains unclear, record the question and ask a teacher or check a reliable reference later. This keeps one problem from consuming the entire session.
